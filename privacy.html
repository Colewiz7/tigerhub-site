TigerHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-03

TigerHub is an unofficial student project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT).

This page explains what the TigerHub app stores on your device and what it contacts over the network. It is short because the app is small.

The short version

  • TigerHub has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It does not ask for or store your RIT username, password, or any other credentials.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • The app does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or in-app purchases.
  • The app does not send your settings, housing area, or any personal data to the developer or to any server the developer runs.

What is stored on your device

  • Your preferences. These include your chosen housing area and, if you enter one, your building or room, your dining pins and hidden spots, diet and allergen filters, event filters and keywords, Campus section order, Today card order, and your color scheme. They are saved locally with the platform's standard preferences storage. A building or room may be personal information, so it is optional and never leaves your device.
  • Cached public data. To work offline, the app saves snapshots of public information (menus, hours, events, and similar) as files in the app's private storage, along with the time each was fetched.

Older installs may also hold a small amount of cached data from an earlier storage method until the app cleans it up. It is the same public data.

What the app contacts over the network

The app fetches public information straight from your device. These are the hosts it calls:

  • www.rit.edu (university events, dining menus, visiting chefs, food trucks, recreation hours)
  • tigercenter.rit.edu (dining locations and hours)
  • maps.rit.edu (campus places and dining occupancy)
  • make.rit.edu (SHED equipment availability)
  • campusgroups.rit.edu (student club events; its feed may redirect to a content delivery network)
  • ritathletics.com (athletics calendar)
  • apiservicelocatorstenantrit.fdmealplanner.com (dining menus from FD MealPlanner)

Map shapes and paths come from OpenStreetMap data bundled inside the app, so the app does not call OpenStreetMap while you use the map. Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

These requests ask for public pages and feeds. They contain the date or a place ID, not your preferences, housing area, or room. No login-protected endpoint is contacted.

What those servers can see

Like any app that loads a web page, each request reveals your device's IP address to the server, and those servers may keep their own logs. The app also sends a User-Agent that identifies it:

TigerHub/0.1.0 (personal non-commercial campus info app; not affiliated with RIT; contact https://github.com/Colewiz7/tigerhub)

It includes the project's public web address, so the sites can reach the developer if the app causes a problem. It does not include any information about you. TigerHub does not control these servers or their logs.

Links that open outside the app

Some rows open other sites in your browser or another app, such as Tiger Spend, SIS, myCourses, eServices, Tiger Chat, the Service Center, facilities requests, Reserve, and online ordering. Some of them ask you to sign in. You sign in on their pages, not in TigerHub, and TigerHub never sees what you enter. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave the app. Phone numbers and email addresses open your phone or mail app.

Permissions

  • Android: the only permission the app declares is Internet access, to load the public data above.
  • iOS: the app asks for no permissions, such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

The app does not use your location.

Children

TigerHub is made for college students and is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling TigerHub removes its preferences and cached files from your device. Because the developer does not receive your data, there is nothing on a developer server to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change too.
