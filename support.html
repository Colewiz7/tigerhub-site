TigerHub Support

TigerHub is a campus info app for RIT students: dining menus and hours, events, a campus map, shuttles, and package pickup, all in one place.

TigerHub is an unofficial student project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT).

FAQ

Where does the data come from?
Public RIT pages and feeds, such as university events, dining menus, and athletics. TigerHub reads them from your phone. If RIT's pages are slow or wrong, the app can lag or be wrong too.
Why does something look out of date?
The app saves the last good copy so it works offline. When a saved copy is showing, the app shows a small note with how old it is (for example "3h ago"). Pull down to refresh when you have a connection.
Is TigerHub official?
No. It is built and maintained by a student and has no connection to RIT. For anything important, check with RIT directly.
Can I sign in or check my balance?
No. TigerHub has no login and never asks for your RIT credentials. Rows like Tiger Spend open RIT's own site in your browser, where you sign in yourself.
How do I find my app version?
Open Settings, then About. The version is listed there.
